# IT Support Guide

## Reporting an incident

Report a suspected security incident immediately through the Security Incident
form in the Help Desk Portal. Include when it happened, which company device or
account may be affected, and what you observed. Do not investigate by deleting
files or resetting a potentially affected device unless Security instructs you
to do so. If you cannot access the portal, call the internal Security hotline.

## Account access

Request access to a company application through the Access Requests section of
the Help Desk Portal. Select the application and business role, explain why
access is needed, and name the manager who approves the request. The application
owner grants access after manager approval. Never share your password or
multi-factor authentication code with a colleague or support agent.

## Laptop problems

Open a hardware ticket in the Help Desk Portal and include the laptop asset tag,
operating system, and a description of the problem. IT Support responds within
one business day. If the laptop cannot start, contact the IT Support desk by
phone and provide the asset tag. Do not attempt to open or repair the laptop
yourself.

## Password reset

Use the company account recovery page to reset a forgotten password. Verify
your identity using the enrolled multi-factor authentication method. IT Support
will never ask you to disclose your current password or a one-time code.
Contact the Help Desk Portal if account recovery does not work.

## Backups

Save work files in the company-managed cloud drive. The drive backs up files
automatically each night and retains deleted files for 30 days. Files stored
only on a local desktop are not included in the managed backup. Contact IT
Support to request recovery of a deleted cloud-drive file.

## Service availability

The guide does not state a guaranteed uptime percentage or a scheduled monthly
maintenance window for company applications.

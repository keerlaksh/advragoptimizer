# Employee Handbook

## Reimbursement

Employees submit reimbursement requests through the Finance Portal. Attach an
itemized receipt for every expense. Submit a request within 30 calendar days of
the purchase. A manager reviews the request first, and Finance issues approved
reimbursements in the next regular payroll cycle.

## Time off

Submit planned time-off requests in the People Portal at least 10 business days
before the first day away. The employee's manager approves or declines the
request in the portal. For unexpected illness, notify the manager before the
employee's normal start time on the first day of absence. Record sick leave in
the People Portal when the employee returns.

## Equipment and support

Contact IT Support through the Help Desk Portal for a company laptop, account
access, or a hardware problem. Include the device asset tag and a short
description of the issue. Do not send passwords, recovery codes, or other
authentication secrets in a support request.

## Work arrangements

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Remote work does not change the normal working hours or the requirement to
protect confidential company information. Store company files only in
company-approved storage.

## Benefits

Eligible full-time employees may enroll in the health plan during their first
30 days of employment or during the annual enrollment period. The handbook
does not specify the monthly employee premium or the names of individual plan
providers.
